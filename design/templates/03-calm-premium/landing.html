Our approach

Built on evidence, not entertainment.

Every lesson on ATAL AI follows an Item Response Theory model — the same psychometric framework used by GRE and JEE coaches to estimate true mastery, not just answer rate.

The mascot is friendly; the science is serious.

Adaptive

Questions adjust to each student's ability in real time. Easy enough to encourage, hard enough to teach.

Offline-first

Lessons cache on the device. A student in Majuli can study even when there's no signal — and sync when they're back online.

Multilingual

Every concept is available in Assamese, Hindi, and English. Voice tutor handles natural pronunciation across all three.
